Hey! I'm an R&D engineer with a build-whatever-it-takes mindset. I play at the intersection of engineering and biology. 


You can find me in lab rolling up my sleeves at the bench, cell culture hood, machine shop, cleanroom, and keyboard. 
  
  
Currently at [Calico Life Sciences](https://www.calicolabs.com/), building practical, high-impact systems that solve real biological bottlenecks.

The goal isn't just to make the hardware faster or just to build cool tools; it's to generate function-first datasets that drive life sciences R&D.
